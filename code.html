Financial Support

Generous Scholarships for International Students

We offer a wide range of scholarships to support high-achieving international students from around the globe in reaching their full potential.

Global Academic Excellence Scholarship

50% Tuition Fee Reduction

For top-tier students with an ATAR equivalent of 98 or above.

South Australian Government Scholarships

Full Study Support

Available for students from select partner regions across Asia and Europe.

Family Scholarships

25% Fee Reduction

For siblings or children of alumni currently enrolled or graduated.

Higher Education Relief Scholarship

One-off $10,000 Grant

Designed for students facing unexpected financial hardship during study.

Global Citizens Scholarship

15% - 30% Fee Reduction

Awarded based on academic merit and community leadership potential.

Science & Tech Excellence Award

$5,000 Annual Stipend

Specifically for STEM students entering Undergraduate programs.

Postgraduate Research Grant

Living Allowance + Fees

For PhD and Masters by Research candidates in key innovation sectors.

EAL Support Scholarship

$2,500 Support Credit

For students requiring additional English language bridging programs.

Find Your Place.

Choosing the right accommodation is a key part of your university experience. We offer a variety of safe, comfortable, and convenient living options to suit every student's needs.

On-Campus Living

University-managed residences like Roseworthy Residential College and Scape Adelaide offer 24/7 support and the ultimate convenience of being just steps away from your classes.

Managed Student Apartments

Experience modern facilities and a vibrant community at partner provider locations like The Village, specifically designed for a balanced student lifestyle.

Private Rental Options

Our accommodation team provides guidance and resources to help you find suitable off-campus housing in Adelaide's city center or surrounding suburbs.
